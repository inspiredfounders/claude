## 2. Information we collect

### Information you give us directly
- **Account details**: email address, password (stored securely and
  hashed by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store
  your raw password), full name.
- **Profile information**: avatar photo, cover photo, bio, company name,
  company stage, industry, location, personal website, LinkedIn and X/Twitter
  handles.
- **Birth data (optional)**: if you choose to enter your date of birth, time
  of birth, and birth city/country, we use it to generate your personal
  "natal chart" and daily astrology-flavoured content within the app. This
  is entirely optional — the app works fully without it. We derive your
  sun/moon/rising sign from this data and store those results too.
- **Community content**: posts, comments, likes, and saves you make in The
  Club community feed, including any photos or video you attach and any
  hashtags you use.
- **Event activity**: RSVPs to events and Assembly sessions you register
  for or attend.
- **Job board activity**: roles you post, and applications you submit
  (which include your name, email, phone number, website, LinkedIn URL, a
  written pitch, and relevant experience — this is shared directly with the
  founder who posted the role).
- **Mentor activity**: if you are a mentor, your mentor profile (bio,
  expertise, social links) and the sessions you host. If you request 1:1
  mentorship from a mentor, we collect what you write about your business,
  your challenge, your goal, your preferred session length, and your
  availability, and share it with that mentor.
- **Connections**: connection requests you send or receive with other
  members.
- **Conversations with Muse**: if you use the "Muse" AI guide feature, the
  messages you send are processed by Anthropic's Claude API to generate a
  response. We send your message text (and the topic you selected) to
  Anthropic for this purpose; we do not send your name, email, or other
  account details as part of that request. See Anthropic's own privacy
  policy for how they handle API data.

### Information collected automatically
- **Push notification tokens**: if you opt in to notifications, we store a
  push subscription (for the web app) or a device token (for the iOS/
  Android app) so we can deliver your daily notification and other alerts.
  You can disable this at any time in Settings.
- **Basic usage records**: we keep a log of when a daily notification was
  sent to you, purely to avoid sending duplicates — we do not currently use
  any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

### Information we do **not** currently collect
We do not currently integrate a payment processor, advertising network, or
third-party analytics tool in the app. If that changes (for example, when
real membership billing is added), this policy will be updated first and
you'll be notified.

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and secure your account, and let you sign in.
- To operate the core features of the app: the community feed, events,
  the job board, mentorship, member directory, and Vault content library.
- To send you notifications you've opted into (daily inspiration, event
  reminders, mentor responses, connection requests, etc. — each is
  individually toggleable in Settings).
- To generate your personal astrology content, if you've provided birth
  data.
- To route your Muse conversations to Anthropic's API and return a
  response to you.
- To moderate community content and keep the Club safe (our admin team can
  review and remove posts/comments that violate our community guidelines).
- To communicate with you about your account or the Club.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for
third-party advertising.

## 4. Who we share information with

- **Supabase** (our database, authentication, and file storage provider) —
  hosts all the data described above.
- **Anthropic** — receives the text of your Muse conversations (not your
  account identity) to generate a response.
- **Other members** — your public profile, posts, and comments are visible
  to other Club members. If you apply for a job or request mentorship, the
  content of that application is shared with the relevant poster/mentor.
- **Apple / Google** — if you use the iOS or Android app, standard
  platform-level data (crash logs, basic device info) may be collected by
  Apple/Google per their own developer platform policies, outside our
  control.
- We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for
  their own marketing purposes.

## 5. Data retention

We retain your account and profile information for as long as your account
is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your
personal information within a reasonable period, except where we're
required to retain it for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention reasons.
Community posts/comments you've authored may be retained in an
anonymized form to preserve the integrity of conversations they're part
of, unless you request full removal.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction**: you can view and edit most of your profile
  information directly in the app (Profile → Edit Profile).
- **Notifications**: every notification type is individually toggleable in
  Settings → Notifications, and push notifications can be fully disabled.
- **Astrology data**: entirely optional; you can add or remove your birth
  data at any time.
- **Account deletion**: contact us at [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] to request
  deletion of your account and associated data.
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws
  like the GDPR (EU/UK) or CCPA/CPRA (California) — including the right to
  access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object to certain
  processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

## 7. Children's privacy

The Inspired Club is intended for founders and professionals and is not
directed at, or knowingly used by, children under 16. We do not knowingly
collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has
provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.
